Every failed payment is a decision, not a retry.

Recover reads a failed payment, works out why it actually failed, and picks the one action most likely to bring the money back: which rail, which moment, which channel, or nothing at all. Measured against the fixed +1h / +24h / +72h schedule most dunning runs on today.

Where the money comes from

Each rung adds exactly one capability to the one above it, on the same held-out payments in the same world. Net rupees, after every attempt fee, message cost and risk penalty.
